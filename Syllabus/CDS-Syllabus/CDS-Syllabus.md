## 1. CDS Examination Architecture & Academy Pathways

The Combined Defence Services (CDS) examination is conducted bi-annually by UPSC for admission into:
- **Indian Military Academy (IMA)**, Dehradun
- **Indian Naval Academy (INA)**, Ezhimala
- **Air Force Academy (AFA)**, Hyderabad
- **Officers Training Academy (OTA)**, Chennai

### Academy Exam Pattern Comparison

| Academy Entry | Subjects Tested | Duration per Subject | Maximum Written Marks | SSB Interview Marks |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **IMA, INA & AFA** | 1. English <br> 2. General Knowledge <br> 3. Elementary Mathematics | 2 Hours <br> 2 Hours <br> 2 Hours | 100 <br> 100 <br> 100 <br> **Total: 300 Marks** | **300 Marks** (5 Days) |
| **OTA (Men & Women)** | 1. English <br> 2. General Knowledge <br> *(Maths Excluded)* | 2 Hours <br> 2 Hours | 100 <br> 100 <br> **Total: 200 Marks** | **300 Marks** (5 Days) |

## 2. English Syllabus for CDS 2025

The English section evaluates candidates' linguistic proficiency, practical vocabulary, contextual comprehension, and professional communication capability.

| Section | Key Topics | Purpose & Scope |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Vocabulary** | • Basic Grammar <br> • Idioms & Phrases <br> • Synonyms & Antonyms <br> • Sentence Completion, etc. | Evaluates precision and range of English lexical knowledge. |
| **Reading Passage** | • Reading Comprehension Solving <br> • Para Jumbles <br> • Understanding Main Idea & Supporting Details <br> • Passage-based questions | Assesses analytical reading, cohesion, and context deduction. |
| **Grammar** | • Error Spotting <br> • Sentence Improvement <br> • Cloze Test <br> • Active & Passive Voice <br> • Direct & Indirect Speech <br> • Transformation of Sentences | Assesses formal grammatical syntax, usage, and structure. |

### OTA English Section Highlights
- Sentence Arrangement & Ordering of Words
- Spotting Errors & Selecting Words
- Synonyms and Antonyms
- Reading Comprehension
- Ordering of Sentences & Para Jumbles
- Idioms and Phrases
- Fill in the Blanks

## 3. Elementary Mathematics Syllabus (For IMA, INA & AFA)

Focuses on standard arithmetic, elementary number theory, algebra, trigonometry, geometry, mensuration, and statistics.

### Question Weightage Breakdown
| Topic Area | Approximate Number of Questions |
| :--- | :---: |
| **Number System & Arithmetic** | **20 – 22 Questions** |
| **Mensuration (2D & 3D)** | **14 – 16 Questions** |
| **Geometry** | **10 – 12 Questions** |
| **Trigonometry** | **10 – 12 Questions** |
| **Algebra** | **10 – 11 Questions** |

### Detailed Topic Specifications

#### 1. Number System
- Natural numbers, Integers, Rational and Real numbers.
- Fundamental operations: addition, subtraction, multiplication, division.
- Square roots, Decimal fractions, Unitary method.
- Time and distance, Time and work.
- Percentages, Simple and Compound Interest applications.
- Profit and loss, Ratio and proportion, Variation.

#### 2. Elementary Number Theory
- Division algorithm, Prime and composite numbers.
- Tests of divisibility by 2, 3, 4, 5, 9, and 11.
- Multiples and factors, Factorization theorem.
- H.C.F. and L.C.M., Euclidean algorithm.
- Logarithms to base 10, Laws of logarithms, Use of logarithmic tables.

#### 3. Algebra
- Basic operations, Simple factors, Remainder theorem, HCF & LCM.
- Theory of polynomials, Solutions of quadratic equations.
- Relations between roots and coefficients.
- Simultaneous linear equations (analytical and graphical solutions).
- Simultaneous linear inequations in two variables and their solutions.
- Practical problems involving equations and inequalities in two variables, or quadratic equations in one variable.
- Set notation and set language.
- Rational expressions and conditional identities, Laws of indices.

#### 4. Trigonometry
- Sine x, cosine x, tan x when zsh^\circ < x < 90^\circ$.
- Values of $\sin x, \cos x, 	an x$ for  = 0^\circ, 30^\circ, 45^\circ, 60^\circ, 90^\circ$.
- Simple trigonometric identities and use of trigonometric tables.
- Simple cases of heights and distances.

#### 5. Geometry
- Lines and angles, Plane and plane figures.
- Theorems on properties of angles at a point, Parallel lines.
- Sides and angles of a triangle, Congruency and similarity of triangles.
- Concurrence of medians and altitudes, Properties of angles.
- Sides and diagonals of parallelograms, rectangles, and squares.
- Circles and their properties including tangents, normals, and loci.

#### 6. Mensuration
- Areas of parallelograms, squares, rectangles, triangles, and circles.
- Areas of figures that can be split up into these figures (Field Book).
- Surface area and volume of right circular cones, cylinders, cuboids, and spheres.

#### 7. Statistics
- Collection and tabulation of statistical data.
- Graphical representation: Frequency polygons, Histograms, Bar charts, Pie charts.
- Measures of central tendency (Mean, Median, Mode).

## 4. General Knowledge Syllabus for CDS 2025

Encompasses current affairs, scientific awareness of daily observations, Indian history, geography, polity, economics, and ecology without requiring specialized technical degrees.

### Subject Modules
1. **Current Affairs:** Events of national and international importance, Sports, Awards & Honours, Books & Authors, Major developments in politics, economics, and social issues.
2. **History:** Ancient, Medieval, and Modern Indian history, Indian National Movement (1857–1947), Growth of nationalism.
3. **Geography:** Physical, Social, and Economic aspects of geography, Indian geography and World geography.
4. **Indian Polity & Governance:** Constitution of India, Political system, Panchayati Raj, Public Policy, Rights Issues, Judicial and administrative frameworks.
5. **Economic & Social Development:** Sustainable Development, Poverty alleviation, Financial inclusion, Demographics, Social sector initiatives.
6. **Science & Technology:** General science developments, Space technology, Biotechnology, Computer & IT technology.
7. **Environmental Ecology & Biodiversity:** Ecological conservation, Pollution and degradation, Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA), Climate change.
8. **Defence & Security Issues:** National security current affairs, Internal and external threat vectors, Role and branches of the Indian Armed Forces, Defence-related gallantry and distinguished service awards.

## 5. CDS SSB Interview & Personality Assessment (300 Marks)

A comprehensive 5-day evaluation process conducted by three independent assessors: the Interviewing Officer (IO), Group Testing Officer (GTO), and Psychologist to assess Officer Like Qualities (OLQ) and trainability.

| Stage | Name of Assessment | Components Included |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Stage 1** | Screening Test | • Officer Intelligence Rating (OIR) <br> • Picture Perception and Description Test (PPDT) & Discussion |
| **Stage 2** | Comprehensive Psychological & Field Evaluation | • Personal Interview with IO <br> • Group Testing Officer (GTO) Tasks (GD, GPE, PGT, HGT, FGT, Snake Race, Lecturette) <br> • Psychological Tests (TAT, WAT, SRT, Self Description) <br> • Final Board Conference |
