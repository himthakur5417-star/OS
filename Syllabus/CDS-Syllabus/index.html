Combined Defence Services Examination (CDS 1 & CDS 2 2025)

The CDS examination serves as the premier graduate gateway for commissioning into the Indian Military Academy (IMA), Indian Naval Academy (INA), Air Force Academy (AFA), and Officers Training Academy (OTA). IMA, INA & AFA require 3 papers (300 Marks), while OTA requires 2 papers (200 Marks, excluding Mathematics), followed by the 300-mark 5-day SSB Evaluation.

📐 Elementary Mathematics Syllabus & Question Weightage

Estimated Topic Weightage (CDS 1 2025 Pattern)

Number System & Arithmetic
20–22 Qs
Natural numbers, Integers, Rational and real numbers. Fundamental operations (addition, subtraction, multiplication, division). Square roots, Decimal fractions, Unitary method, Time and distance, Time and work, Percentages, Applications to simple and compound interest, Profit and loss, Ratio and proportion, Variation.
Elementary Number Theory
Fundamental Theory
Division algorithm, Prime and composite numbers, Tests of divisibility by 2, 3, 4, 5, 9, and 11. Multiples and factors, Factorization theorem, H.C.F. and L.C.M., Euclidean algorithm, Logarithms to base 10, laws of logarithms, Use of logarithmic tables.
Algebra
10–11 Qs
Basic operations, Simple factors, Remainder theorem, HCF & LCM, Theory of polynomials, Solutions of quadratic equations, Relations between roots and coefficients, Simultaneous linear equations (analytical and graphical), Simultaneous linear inequations in two variables, Set notation and language, Rational expressions and conditional identities, Laws of indices.
Trigonometry
10–12 Qs
Sine x, cosine x, Tan x when 0° < x < 90°. Values of sin x, cos x & tan x for x = 0°, 30°, 45°, 60° & 90°. Simple trigonometric identities. Use of trigonometric tables. Simple cases of heights and distances.
Geometry
10–12 Qs
Lines and angles, Plane and plane figures, Theorems on properties of angles at a point, Parallel lines, Sides and angles of a triangle, Congruency and similarity of triangles, Concurrence of medians and altitudes, Properties of angles, Sides and diagonals of parallelograms, rectangles, and squares, Circles and properties including tangents, normals, and loci.
Mensuration
14–16 Qs
Areas of parallelograms, squares, rectangles, triangles, and circles. Areas of figures split into these figures (Field Book). Lateral surface and volume of right circular cones and cylinders. Surface area and volume of cuboids, and surface area and volume of spheres.
Statistics
Data & Central Tendency
Collection and tabulation of statistical data, Graphical representation: frequency polygons, Histograms, Bar charts, Pie charts, etc. Measures of central tendency (mean, median, mode).
📖 English Syllabus for CDS 2025
Mandatory for All Cadets · 100 Marks
Section Topics Covered Description & Objective
Vocabulary Basic Grammar, Idioms & Phrases, Synonyms & Antonyms, Sentence Completion, etc. Tests the candidate’s range, depth, and mastery of English vocabulary.
Reading Passage Reading Comprehension Solving, Para Jumbles, Understanding main idea and supporting details, Answering questions based on the passage, etc. Tests the candidate’s ability to understand, parse, and interpret written English under time constraints.
Grammar Error Spotting, Sentence Improvement, Cloze test, Active and passive voice, Direct and indirect speech, Transformation of sentences, etc. Evaluates rigorous formal knowledge of English grammar rules and syntactic structures.

🎖️ CDS Syllabus 2025 for OTA (Officers Training Academy)
English + GK Only · 200 Marks Total
OTA Entry Pathway Architecture

The syllabus for OTA aligns closely with IMA and INA with one crucial distinction: the Mathematics paper is excluded. The examination consists solely of English (100 Marks) and General Knowledge (100 Marks), for a combined written total of 200 marks.

⚔️ CDS SSB Interview & Selection Process (300 Marks)
5-Day Evaluation · 3 Independent Assessors
SSB Stage Assigned Assessments Focus & Evaluation Criteria
Stage 1 (Screening) • Officer Intelligence Rating (OIR)
• Picture Perception and Description Test (PPDT) & Group Discussion 		Verbal & non-verbal reasoning, cognitive alertness, and immediate screening.
Stage 2 (Comprehensive) • Personal Interview with Interviewing Officer (IO)
• Group Testing Officer (GTO) Tasks (GD, GPE, PGT, HGT, FGT, Snake Race, Lecturette)
• Psychology Test (TAT, WAT, SRT, Self Description) 		Detailed appraisal of Officer Like Qualities (OLQ), trainability, leadership, emotional stability, and team coordination.
Stage 3 / Final Day • Final Board Conference
• Group Discussion & Cumulative Assessors Review 		Consolidated decision by IO, GTO, and Psychologist on candidate recommendation.
