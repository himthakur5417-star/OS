## 2. Paper 1: Mathematics (Code 01)
- **Total Questions:** 120 Questions
- **Total Marks:** 300 Marks
- **Marks per Correct:** +2.5 Marks
- **Negative Marking:** -0.83 Marks per wrong answer
- **Time Allowed:** 2 Hours 30 Minutes
- **Academic Benchmark:** Standard of Class 11 & Class 12 NCERT Mathematics

### Estimated Question Weightage Distribution
| Topic Area | Approx. Questions | Marks Weightage |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| **Calculus (Differential & Integral)** | 20 – 25 | 50.0 – 62.5 Marks |
| **Matrices & Determinants** | ~30 | 75.0 Marks |
| **Trigonometry & Inverse Trig** | ~30 | 75.0 Marks |
| **Quadratic Equations & Complex Numbers** | 25 – 35 | 62.5 – 87.5 Marks |
| **Probability & Statistics** | ~10 | 25.0 Marks |
| **Vectors & Analytical Geometry (2D/3D)** | 15 – 20 | 37.5 – 50.0 Marks |

### Chapter-Wise Mathematics Syllabus Breakdown
1. **Algebra:**
   - Sets, subsets, Venn diagrams, De Morgan’s laws, Cartesian product, relations, equivalence relations.
   - Real numbers, Complex numbers, Modulus, argument, cube roots of unity.
   - Conversion of numbers from Binary system to Decimals and vice-versa.
   - Arithmetic Progressions (AP), Geometric Progressions (GP), Harmonic Progressions (HP).
   - Quadratic equations with real coefficients, Linear inequations.
   - Permutations and Combinations, Binomial theorem and its applications, Logarithms and their properties.

2. **Matrices and Determinants:**
   - Types of matrices, matrix operations (addition, scalar multiplication, matrix multiplication).
   - Determinant of a square matrix, basic properties of determinants.
   - Adjoint and inverse of a square matrix.
   - Applications: Solution of systems of linear equations in two or three unknowns by Cramer’s rule and Matrix inverse method.

3. **Trigonometry:**
   - Angles and measurement in degrees and radians.
   - Trigonometric ratios and standard trigonometric identities.
   - Sum and difference formulae, Multiple and sub-multiple angles.
   - Inverse trigonometric functions.
   - Applications: Heights and distances, properties of triangles.

4. **Analytical Geometry (2D and 3D):**
   - **Two Dimensions:** Rectangular Cartesian coordinate system, distance formula, section formula. Equation of a line in slope, intercept, normal, and general forms. Angle between two lines, distance of a point from a line. Standard equation of a circle, parabola, ellipse, and hyperbola. Eccentricity and conic axes.
   - **Three Dimensions:** Coordinate axes and planes in 3D, distance between two points, direction cosines and direction ratios. Equation of a plane and straight line in various forms. Angle between two planes and angle between two lines. Sphere equation.

5. **Differential Calculus:**
   - Real-valued functions, domain, range, and graph. Composite functions, one-to-one, onto, and inverse functions.
   - Limits, standard limits, continuity, algebraic operations on continuous functions.
   - Derivative of a function at a point, geometrical and physical interpretation. Derivatives of sum, product, quotient, composite, and implicit functions. Second-order derivatives.
   - Applications: Rate of change, increasing and decreasing functions, maxima and minima.

6. **Integral Calculus & Differential Equations:**
   - Integration as inverse of differentiation, integration by substitution and by parts. Standard integrals involving algebraic, trigonometric, exponential, and hyperbolic functions.
   - Definite integrals and determination of areas bounded by plane curves.
   - Order and degree of differential equations, formation of differential equations.
   - General and particular solutions, first-order first-degree equations. Applications in growth and decay models.

7. **Vector Algebra:**
   - Vectors in 2D and 3D space, magnitude and direction. Unit and null vectors.
   - Vector addition, scalar multiplication, dot (scalar) product, cross (vector) product.
   - Applications: Work done by a force, moment of a force, and geometrical problems.

8. **Statistics & Probability:**
   - **Statistics:** Frequency distribution, cumulative frequency, graphical representation (histograms, polygons, ogives). Measures of central tendency (mean, median, mode). Measures of dispersion (range, mean deviation, standard deviation, variance).
   - **Probability:** Random experiments, sample space, events (mutually exclusive, exhaustive, independent). Classical and statistical definition. Conditional probability, Bayes theorem. Random variables, Binomial distribution.

## 3. Paper 2: General Ability Test (GAT) (Code 02)
- **Total Questions:** 150 Questions
- **Total Marks:** 600 Marks
- **Marks per Correct:** +4.0 Marks
- **Negative Marking:** -1.33 Marks per wrong answer
- **Time Allowed:** 2 Hours 30 Minutes
- **Structure:** Part A (English - 200 Marks) + Part B (General Knowledge - 400 Marks)

### Part A: English (50 Questions | 200 Marks)
Assesses verbal precision, command over English syntax, vocabulary depth, and contextual understanding.
- Spotting of Errors
- Sentence Improvement & Correction
- Grammar & Usage (Prepositions, Tenses, Conjunctions, Articles, Subject-Verb Agreement)
- Vocabulary, Synonyms & Antonyms
- Idioms, Proverbs & Phrasal Verbs
- Fill in the Blanks & Cloze Test
- Para Jumbling / Sentence Rearrangement
- Reading Comprehension & Cohesion
- Sentence & Paragraph Completion

### Part B: General Knowledge & Sciences (100 Questions | 400 Marks)

#### Section 1: Physics (~25 Questions | ~100 Marks)
- Physical Properties and States of Matter, Mass, Weight, Volume, Density, Specific Gravity.
- Archimedes Principle, Fluid Pressure, Barometer.
- Motion, Velocity, Acceleration, Newton’s Laws of Motion, Force, Linear Momentum.
- Parallelogram of Forces, Stability and Equilibrium of bodies, Gravitation.
- Work, Power, Energy, Kinetic and Potential Energy.
- Heat and Temperature, Measurement of Heat, Transference of Heat (Conduction, Convection, Radiation), Effects of Heat.
- Sound Waves, Propagation of Sound, Echo, Simple Musical Instruments.
- Light, Rectilinear Propagation, Reflection, Refraction, Spherical Mirrors, Lenses, Human Eye.
- Magnetism: Natural and Artificial Magnets, Magnetic Field Lines, Earth as a Magnet.
- Current Electricity: Static & Dynamic, Conductors and Insulators, Ohm’s Law, Simple Circuits, Heating/Lighting/Magnetic effects of current, Electric Power.
- Practical Working Principles: Simple Pendulum, Pulleys, Siphons, Levers, Balloons, Pumps, Hydrometer, Pressure Cooker, Thermos Flask, Gramophone, Telegraph, Telephone, Periscope, Telescope, Microscope, Mariner’s Compass, Lightning Conductors, Safety Fuses.

#### Section 2: Chemistry (~15 Questions | ~60 Marks)
- Physical and Chemical Changes.
- Elements, Mixtures, and Chemical Compounds.
- Chemical Symbols, Formulae, and Balanced Equations.
- Laws of Chemical Combination (excluding mathematical problems).
- Properties of Air and Water.
- Preparation, Properties & Uses of Hydrogen, Oxygen, Nitrogen, Carbon Dioxide.
- Oxidation and Reduction reactions.
- Acids, Bases, and Salts.
- Allotropes and forms of Carbon.
- Natural and Artificial Fertilizers.
- Industrial materials: Soap, Glass, Ink, Paper, Cement, Paints, Safety Matches, Gunpowder.
- Atomic Structure, Atomic and Molecular Weights, Valency.

#### Section 3: General Science & Biology (~10 Questions | ~40 Marks)
- Difference between Living and Non-living things.
- Food: Constituents, Nutrients (Carbohydrates, Proteins, Fats, Vitamins, Minerals), Balanced Diet.
- Growth and Reproduction in Plants and Animals.
- Elementary Anatomy and Physiology of the Human Body and vital organ systems.
- Common Epidemics, Communicable and Non-communicable diseases, their causes, vectors, and prevention.

#### Section 4: History, Freedom Struggle & Polity (~20 Questions | ~80 Marks)
- Forces Shaping the Modern World: Renaissance, Age of Exploration and Discovery.
- American War of Independence, French Revolution, Industrial Revolution, Russian Revolution.
- Survey of Indian History with focus on Culture, Heritage, and Civilisation.
- Freedom Movement in India (1857 to 1947), Leaders, Movements, and Milestones.
- Elementary Study of Indian Constitution, Fundamental Rights, Directive Principles, Administration.
- Panchayati Raj, Co-operatives, Community Development.
- Post-Independence India: Five-Year Plans, Bhoodan, Sarvodaya, National Integration.
- Basic Teachings and Philosophy of Mahatma Gandhi.
- United Nations, Panchsheel, Non-Aligned Movement, Democracy, Socialism, and Global Relations.

#### Section 5: Geography (~20 Questions | ~80 Marks)
- Earth: Shape, size, rotation, revolution, latitudes, longitudes, international date line, time zones.
- Earth Movements, Origin of Earth, Internal Structure, Rocks and their classification.
- Weathering (Mechanical and Chemical), Earthquakes, Volcanoes.
- Ocean Currents, Tides, Marine Relief.
- Atmosphere: Composition, Structure, Atmospheric Pressure, Planetary Winds, Cyclones, Anticyclones, Humidity, Condensation, Precipitation.
- Major Climatic and Natural Regions of the World.
- Indian Geography: Physiography, Climate, Monsoons, Rivers, Soils, Natural Vegetation, Mineral and Energy Resources, Agriculture, Industries.
- Major Seaports, Trade Routes (Sea, Land, Air), Import and Export Patterns of India.

#### Section 6: Current Affairs (~10–12 Questions | ~40 Marks)
- Important national and international events of recent 6–12 months.
- Prominent National & Global Personalities in News.
- Defence developments, new acquisitions, joint military exercises, defence technology.
- Major summits, geopolitical treaties, international organizations.
- Awards, Honours, Sports championships, cultural festivals.
