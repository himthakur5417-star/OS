National Defence Academy & Naval Academy Examination (NDA & NA)

The NDA written exam is conducted bi-annually by the Union Public Service Commission (UPSC). The written test comprises two mandatory papers: Paper 1 (Mathematics - 300 Marks) and Paper 2 (General Ability Test - 600 Marks) for a total of 900 marks, followed by the 900-mark SSB Interview for qualifying cadets.

📐 Paper 1: Mathematics Syllabus
120 Questions · 300 Marks · Class 11 & 12

Topic-Wise Question Distribution (Historical Pattern)

Matrices & Determinants 30 Qs
Trigonometry 30 Qs
Calculus (Diff & Int) 20–25 Qs
Quadratic Equations & Algebra 15–20 Qs
Complex Numbers 10–15 Qs
Probability & Statistics 10 Qs
Algebra
High Frequency
Sets, Venn diagrams, De Morgan laws, Cartesian product, relation, equivalence relation. Real numbers, Complex numbers, Modulus, Cube roots, Conversion of a number in a Binary system to Decimals, and vice-versa. Arithmetic, Geometric, and Harmonic progressions. Quadratic equations, Linear inequations, Permutation and Combination, Binomial theorem, and Logarithms.
Differential Calculus
Class 11 & 12
Concept of a real-valued function, domain, range, and graph of a function. Composite functions, one-to-one, onto, and inverse functions. Notion of limit, Standard limits, Continuity of functions, algebraic operations on continuous functions. Derivative at a point, geometrical and physical interpretation. Derivatives of sum, product, quotient, composite function, second-order derivatives. Increasing/decreasing functions, Maxima and minima.
Matrices & Determinants
~30 Questions
Types of matrices, operations on matrices. Determinant of a matrix, basic properties of determinants. Adjoint and inverse of a square matrix. Applications: Solution of a system of linear equations in two or three unknowns by Cramer’s rule and by Matrix Method.
Integral Calculus & Diff Equations
Core Calculus
Integration as inverse of differentiation, substitution and parts, standard integrals involving algebraic, trigonometric, exponential, and hyperbolic functions. Evaluation of definite integrals and areas of plane regions bounded by curves. Order and degree of differential equations, formation of differential equations, general and particular solutions, first-order first-degree equations. Applications in growth and decay.
Trigonometry
~30 Questions
Angles and measures in degrees and radians. Trigonometric ratios. Trigonometric identities, Sum and difference formulae. Multiple and Sub-multiple angles. Inverse trigonometric functions. Applications: Height and distance, properties of triangles.
Vector Algebra
2D & 3D Vectors
Vectors in two and three dimensions, magnitude and direction. Unit and null vectors, addition of vectors, scalar multiplication, scalar (dot) product, vector (cross) product. Applications: work done by a force and moment of a force and geometrical problems.
Analytical Geometry (2D & 3D)
Co-ordinates & Conics
Cartesian Coordinate system, Distance formula, Equation of a line in various forms. Angle between two lines, distance of a point from a line. Standard forms of circle, parabola, ellipse, and hyperbola. Eccentricity and axis of a conic. 3D space: Distance between two points, Direction Cosines and direction ratios. Equation of planes and lines in various forms. Angle between two lines and two planes. Equation of a sphere.
Statistics and Probability
Applied Math
Probability: Random experiment, sample space, mutually exclusive and exhaustive events, impossible and certain events. Union and Intersection of events. Classical and statistical probability. Conditional probability, Bayes’ theorem. Random variables, Binomial distribution and applications.

Pressure Barometer, Human Eye Rectilinear propagation of Light, Reflection and Refraction
Motion of objects, Velocity and Acceleration Spherical mirrors and Lenses

Oxidation and Reduction, Acids, bases, and salts Fertilizers—Natural and Artificial

🌱 Paper 2 Part B: General Science & Biology
Basic Living Science · ~10 Questions
Living Organisms & Physiology
Difference between the living and non-living world. Growth, reproduction, and biological cycles in Plants and Animals. Elementary knowledge of the Human Body, vital organ systems, respiratory, circulatory, nervous, and digestive mechanisms.
Nutrition, Diet & Health
Food as a prime source of energy for human physiology. Constituents of food (carbohydrates, proteins, fats, vitamins, minerals, water). Concept and requirements of a Balanced Diet across age and activity groups.
Epidemics & Disease Prevention
Common epidemics, infectious and vector-borne diseases, their environmental causes, transmission routes, sanitation protocols, immunization, and medical preventive measures.
🏛️ Paper 2 Part B: History, Freedom Movement & Polity
Culture & Constitution · ~20 Questions
World History & Revolutions Indian Heritage, Constitution & Society
Forces shaping the modern world: Renaissance, Exploration & Discovery A broad survey of Indian History, with emphasis on Culture and Civilisation
War of American Independence, French Revolution, Industrial Revolution, Russian Revolution Freedom Movement in India (1857 to 1947), Leaders & Milestones
Impact of Science and Technology on Society Elementary Study of Indian Constitution, Fundamental Rights, Administration
Concept of One World; United Nations, Panchsheel Elementary knowledge of Five Year Plans of India, Economic Reforms
Democracy, Socialism and Communism Ideologies Panchayati Raj, Co-operatives and Community Development
Role of India in the Present World Geopolitics Bhoodan, Sarvodaya, National Integration, and Welfare State Principles
Core Philosophical Thought: Basic Teachings, Non-violence, and Satyagraha principles of Mahatma Gandhi and their modern relevance.
🌍 Paper 2 Part B: Geography & Current Affairs
Physical, Regional & World Events
Earth & Planetary Systems
The Earth, its shape and size. Latitudes and Longitudes, Concept of time zones, International Date Line. Movements of Earth (rotation & revolution) and their seasonal effects. Origin of Earth, internal structure, Rocks and their petrological classification.
Atmosphere & Oceans
Atmosphere composition, layers, Temperature and Atmospheric Pressure, Planetary Winds, Cyclones and Anticyclones, Humidity, Condensation, Precipitation. Ocean Currents, Tides, Marine relief. Types of climate and major natural regions of the world.
Geography of India
Regional Geography of India: Climate, Monsoons, Natural vegetation, Minerals and Power resources. Location and distribution of agricultural and industrial activities. Important Seaports and main sea, land, and air routes of India. Main items of Imports and Exports.
Current Affairs & Defence Events
Significant national and international events of current importance in India and across the world. Prominent personalities, international summits, bilateral defense agreements, military exercises, sports championships, scientific milestones, and cultural awards.
