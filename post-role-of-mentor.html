Five roles a mentor plays in your life
01

The Mirror

Shows you who you really are — your blind spots, strengths, and the gap between where you are and where you think you are.

02

The Compass

Gives you direction when you're overwhelmed. Cuts through noise and tells you exactly what to focus on — and what to ignore.

03

The Anchor

Holds you steady during failure, self-doubt, and the months where nothing seems to work. Their belief keeps you moving.

04

The Accelerator

Compresses years of trial-and-error into weeks. Their experience becomes your shortcut — you skip mistakes they already made.

05

The Standard

Raises the bar of what you consider "enough." A mentor doesn't accept your excuses — they hold you to a version of yourself you haven't met yet.
